# Thin input (not a ready story)

Operator problem: inbound goods sometimes sit waiting (not put away). The
next shift can see that something is waiting, but finance cannot see the extra
cost of that wait (storage, handling, demurrage-style charges). People send
the amounts in chat or email.

Wanted: when a delivery is waiting, the operator can record that it is waiting
and attach a money amount as an expense tied to that delivery, so finance and
the warehouse share one picture — not two side channels.

Not thought through yet: is the amount optional, who is the submitting user,
can you record cost without a delivery id, list vs single-record view, max
remark length, whether gateway routes change.

No service names. No AC. No tracker.
